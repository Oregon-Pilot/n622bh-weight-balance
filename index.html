Important operating notes

This calculator is an aid, not a substitute for the current aircraft weight-and-balance records, loading graph, placards, or pilot judgment. Verify all inputs and results against the current aircraft documents before every flight. The POH warns that CG can move aft as fuel is burned; check landing CG as well as takeoff CG. The POH also states that the baggage compartment must not be used in the utility category.

The app checks the stated 1,800 lb gross weight, 75 lb baggage limit, and 77.95–84.84 in CG range. It does not model any additional loading-envelope curves or category-specific limitations.
